The Problem

AI training is broken.
Here's why.

⚠

Tools, Not Thinking

Current programs teach "click here, type this." They skip the hard part: how to question, validate, and contextualize what AI produces.

⚠

No Override Training

Nobody teaches professionals when to reject AI recommendations. In high-stakes decisions, this is the most dangerous gap.

⚠

Badges, Not Judgment

Certifications measure tool usage, not decision quality. A badge that everyone passes proves nothing to employers.

⚠

No Failure Training

Programs showcase AI successes. None teach the failure patterns — when models hallucinate, when data is biased, when automation creates cascading errors.
